FeaturedCapstone project · 2026

ARMS

Academic Records Management System

An AI-enabled academic records system for Northern Paramedical & Technological College of Panabo (NPTCI). Teachers upload class records, ARMS checks every grade against DepEd rules, the registrar validates them, and school heads see clear dashboards and forecasts.

  • Four role-based portals: Administrator, School Head, Registrar and Teacher
  • An XGBoost grade forecaster that predicts the next grading periods with a 90% likely range
  • Poro AI, a local language model run through Ollama, rewrites each class brief in plain words
